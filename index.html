🎩 Petrossian Bar Bellagio

10
🏙️ El Cortez Fremont Street

The oldest original casino in Vegas, untouched and perfect. Penny slots, cheap tables, zero pretension, maximum authenticity. A required Fremont pilgrimage.

💡 PRO TIPS: Walk the strip north on one side, south on the other. Use rideshare between Mandalay Bay and the main strip cluster. Wynn/Encore are a longer haul north — worth it. Fremont is a 10-min Uber from mid-strip and a completely different vibe — plan one dedicated night.
🏀 March Madness in Vegas

There is no better place on earth to watch the tournament. Every sportsbook goes wall-to-wall. You'll be there for the Sweet 16 weekend — one of the best windows of the whole tournament. Ranked best to solid backup.

Insider Playbook
⏰Arrive early on game days. Best seats at Circa, Westgate, and Caesars fill fast during Sweet 16. Get there 30–45 min before tip or you're standing.
📱Fund your betting app before you go. FanDuel, DraftKings, and BetMGM all have Nevada mobile apps. Have your account loaded so you're not at a kiosk while games tip off.
🥃Bourbon + sportsbook = the move. Most major books have full bar service. Order a Blanton's or Weller and settle in. You're in Vegas — don't drink light beer and watch on a 32-inch TV like you're home.
🍺Beer Park at Paris is a solid casual option right on the strip — rooftop patio, cold beers, fountain views, every game on screen. Good afternoon spot between serious sessions.
🚗Circa is worth the Uber. Off-strip in Downtown Vegas, but Stadium Swim is the single greatest March Madness venue in the world. Make the trip at least once — maybe pair it with a Fremont night.
